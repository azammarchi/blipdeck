# Code of conduct

Blipdeck is a small, friendly project, and everyone who takes part in it (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this repository) is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new or whose first language isn't English.
- Be respectful of different opinions and experiences, and keep disagreements about the work, not the person.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing conversations, or spamming.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@azammarchi](https://github.com/azammarchi), may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a problem, mention @azammarchi in a comment, or, for anything you'd rather not raise in public, use GitHub's [**Report content**](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option on the comment or profile involved. Reports will be handled fairly and, as far as possible, confidentially.
